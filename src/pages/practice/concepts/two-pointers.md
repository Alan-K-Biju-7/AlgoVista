# Two Pointers

**Intuition**
Use two indices that move through the array in a controlled way instead of restarting a new scan every time.

**Common shapes**
- Left and right pointers moving inward
- Slow and fast pointers moving forward
- Fixed anchor plus two moving pointers

**When to use it**
- Sorted arrays
- Palindrome checks
- Pair and triplet search
- Area or distance optimization

**Pitfalls**
- Moving the wrong pointer
- Forgetting to skip duplicates
- Losing sorted-order assumptions

**Visual cue**
Highlight both pointers on the array and animate pointer movement after each comparison.
